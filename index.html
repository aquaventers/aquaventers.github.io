Case Study

Craft Candles Website

Project Goal

To create a visually atmospheric website for a handmade candle brand that highlights the product’s mood, warmth, and aesthetic appeal, while maintaining a clean and modern presentation.

Design Approach

A soft, minimalistic approach with warm tones and calm composition was used to reflect the handcrafted nature of the product. The focus was placed on creating an emotional connection through visuals and simplicity.

Key Features

Structure

A clear and linear structure that guides the user through the brand story, product presentation, and key information without overload. Each block is separated with enough white space to maintain readability.

UX

Simple and intuitive scrolling experience with no distractions. The layout allows users to naturally explore the content and focus on the products.

Visual Decisions

Warm color palette combined with neutral backgrounds to highlight the candles. Large imagery and stylish typography emphasize the aesthetic and handmade feel of the brand.

Product Details Mockup
Brand Story Mockup

Result

The website creates a calm and aesthetic space, where the product becomes the central focus. It helps communicate the brand’s atmosphere and presents the candles as a lifestyle element rather than just a product.

Case Study

Expert / Service Landing Page

Expert Landing Page Main Mockup

Project Goal

Design a nicely laid out landing page for a photographer, showcasing his recent work and encouraging users to follow him.

Design Approach

A balance between clarity and modern aesthetics was used to ensure the information is easy to follow while still maintaining a visually appealing layout.

Key Features

Structure

The page is built with a logical flow: introduction → value → details → call to action. Each section gradually leads the user closer to the final decision.

UX

The layout is designed to reduce friction and make navigation effortless. Information is broken into digestible sections, helping users quickly understand the offer.

Visual Decisions

Clean layout with accent elements to highlight key points. Typography and spacing were used to create hierarchy and guide attention through the page.

Result

The landing page presents the service in a clear and structured way, helping users quickly understand the offer and move toward a decision without confusion.
